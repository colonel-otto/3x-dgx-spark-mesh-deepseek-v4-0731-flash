# 2v3 analysis, 2026-08-29 — what the data supports, and what it does not

**Scope.** A configuration-identical TP=3 arm was measured on 2026-08-29 under the
pre-registration in [`PREREGISTRATION-2V3-MATCHED.md`](PREREGISTRATION-2V3-MATCHED.md).
**The matching TP=2 arm was not run** — the orchestrator died during the cluster
transition (cause in §5). So this document does **not** deliver a 2-vs-3 verdict.

What it does deliver is a re-examination of the *published* 2v3 comparison using the raw
per-rep data already in this repository. That re-examination is decisive, and it does not
depend on the missing arm.

---

## 1. Headline: the published 2v3 decode deltas do not survive a significance test

Exact two-sided Mann-Whitney U on the committed per-rep values in
[`20260827-decode-2v3-fixed`](../results/20260827-decode-2v3-fixed/), n=7 vs n=7, α=0.05:

| Depth | TP=3 median | TP=2 median | Published delta | p | Verdict |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 2,048 | 54.30 | 46.53 | **+16.7 %** | 0.0973 | **not significant** |
| 8,192 | 52.87 | 46.29 | +14.2 % | **0.0006** | **significant** |
| 32,768 | 51.98 | 46.81 | +11.0 % | 0.0728 | **not significant** |
| 131,072 | 47.65 | 44.40 | +7.3 % | 0.5350 | **not significant** |
| 262,144 | 48.83 | 44.40 | +10.0 % | 0.2086 | **not significant** |

**Four of five rows are not statistically significant.** The `+16.7 %` figure quoted as
the headline three-node win does not reach significance (p = 0.097). The `+7.3 %` at 131K
— stated in the README as a win across all depths — is p = 0.535, indistinguishable from
a coin flip.

### Why: the deltas are smaller than the arms' own spreads

| Depth | Delta | TP=3 spread | TP=2 spread |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2,048 | +16.7 % | **48.5 %** | 15.6 % |
| 8,192 | +14.2 % | 21.6 % | 17.2 % |
| 32,768 | +11.0 % | 27.1 % | 20.2 % |
| 131,072 | +7.3 % | **41.3 %** | 18.2 % |
| 262,144 | +10.0 % | 29.4 % | 6.6 % |

Every delta is smaller than the spread of at least one arm it was computed from. The 2K
cell that anchors the headline spans **38.22 – 64.54 tok/s** across seven reps.

### The one row that survives

8K is a genuine separation: the distributions do not overlap at all.

```
tp3 8K: 49.90  50.12  50.47  52.87  56.96  60.59  61.34
tp2 8K: 40.89  42.29  44.49  46.29  46.89  47.37  48.84
        tp3 min 49.90  >  tp2 max 48.84
```

**But it still is not a node-count result** — see §2.

## 2. The published arms differ in three engine settings, not one

Read from the live env files and the running engine on 2026-08-29:

| Knob | TP=2 arm | TP=3 arm |
|---|---:|---:|
| `MAX_NUM_SEQS` | 16 | **32** |
| `MTP_NUM_TOKENS` | 5 | **2** |
| `GPU_MEMORY_UTILIZATION` | 0.80 | **0.835** |

The first two were disclosed. **The third was not**, in either `DECISIONS.md` or
`BENCHMARK-2V3-NODES.md`; per Issue #25 it alone is worth ~35 % of the KV pool and
−10.7 % starvation TTFT. So even the significant 8K row measures *"three nodes at one
configuration versus two nodes at another"*, not node count.

## 3. TP=3 reproduces across two days — the *measurement* is sound, the comparison is not

Same configuration, 48 hours apart:

| Depth | Published 08-27 | Spread | This run 08-29 | Spread | Δ |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2,048 | 54.30 | 48.5 % | 51.06 | **9.5 %** | −6.0 % |
| 8,192 | 52.87 | 21.6 % | 51.50 | **6.6 %** | −2.6 % |
| 32,768 | 51.98 | 27.1 % | 52.20 | **5.9 %** | +0.4 % |
| 131,072 | 47.65 | 41.3 % | 46.87 | 30.6 % | −1.6 % |
| 262,144 | 48.83 | 29.4 % | 44.92 | 22.3 % | −8.0 % |

Medians agree within ~±8 %, i.e. inside the combined noise. **Spreads improved by 2–5×**
on the shallow cells, because this run cooled all nodes to ≤70 °C first and sampled clocks
throughout. The published numbers are not *wrong*; they are *imprecise*, and the
imprecision is larger than the effect being claimed.

## 4. Supporting measurements from the matched TP=3 arm

**Concurrency @ 8K, `MTP=2`** (n=5, 2 warm batches per shape):

| cc | Aggregate | Spread | TTFT | Acceptance | Accepted len |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 | 41.07 | 0.69 | 11.07 s | 67.1 % | 1.343 |
| 8 | 49.08 | 2.12 | 18.82 s | 67.7 % | 1.353 |
| 16 | 54.12 | 1.20 | 34.19 s | 66.7 % | 1.334 |

Acceptance is stable at **66.7–67.7 %**, mean accepted length **1.33–1.35** — matching
Issue #32's 66.3 %. Note this contradicts the 76.7–80.4 % figure quoted in
`BENCHMARK-2V3-NODES.md` §D, which came from a single-stream long-horizon probe; at
concurrency the rate is ~67 %.

**Clock envelope** (690 samples/node across the whole arm):

| Node | Clock mean | Min | Max | Temp mean | Max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| sparkmain | 2440 MHz | 2359 | 2522 | 78 °C | 87 |
| spark1 | 2469 MHz | 2314 | 2548 | 81 °C | 89 |
| spark2 | 2426 MHz | 2255 | 2496 | 83 °C | 89 |

**Never reaches 3003 MHz.** See [`GPU-CLOCKS-NOT-LOCKABLE.md`](GPU-CLOCKS-NOT-LOCKABLE.md).

**Hygiene — the TP=3 arm is verified uncontaminated.** The arm logged
`EXCLUSIVITY_FAIL ... 198 foreign requests`; that was an **orchestrator ledger bug, not
contamination**, confirmed four independent ways rather than by assertion:

1. **Request accounting closes exactly.** Read from `rep_details` in the raw
   `concurrency.json`: cc=4 → 20 measured + 8 warm, cc=8 → 40 + 16, cc=16 → 80 + 32
   = 196, plus 2 single-stream warmups = **198**. With the depth sweep's 35 reps + 10
   warmups = 45, the total is **243** — precisely the observed delta, leaving **zero**
   unexplained requests. The ledger had counted only the depth sweep.
2. **The only local client was down for the whole arm.** `open-webui` stopped
   19:40:16, restored 20:39:59; every measurement falls inside that window.
3. **Window integrity: 35/35 reps returned exactly 256 completion tokens**, and 35/35
   had `cached_tokens = 0` (no APC contamination).
4. **No anomalous reps.** Concurrency aggregates cluster tightly
   (cc=16: 53.1/54.3/54.2/54.1/53.6).

For contrast, what real contamination looks like in this repository: the *published*
`20260825-decode-2v3` TP=2 arm contains **bimodal** reps — 428.4/429.8 against a
279–305 cluster at cc=8, and 686.9/687.8 against 462–485 at cc=16, each pair ~40 % above
its own distribution. That signature is **absent** from this arm.

KV pool this boot: 4,688,072 tokens / 4.47x (init log).

## 4b. FIRST MATCHED MEASUREMENT — KV pool, both arms, same instrument

Captured 2026-08-30 03:03 UTC from each arm's own init log, both at `MTP=2` and the full
Profile B configuration (all six settings matched — see the handoff):

| | TP=2 | TP=3 | Ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GPU KV cache size (init log) | **2,217,166 tokens** | **4,688,072 tokens** | **2.11×** |
| Max concurrency @ 1M tokens/request | 2.11× | 4.47× | 2.12× |

**The KV advantage is real and now confirmed under matched configuration — but it is
2.11×, not the 2.6× the README and `BENCHMARK-2V3-NODES.md` claim.**

The published 2.6× (4,457,627 vs 1,711,307) came from arms that differed in
`GPU_MEMORY_UTILIZATION` (0.80 vs 0.835) and `MTP_NUM_TOKENS` (5 vs 2), both of which move
the KV pool: a lower memory fraction shrinks it directly, and a deeper MTP draft enlarges
the CUDA-graph capture that competes with it. Matching those shrinks the ratio by ~19 %.

This is the **first** figure in this repository comparing the two node counts under a
genuinely identical engine configuration. It is also unaffected by every noise problem
that sank the decode comparison — it is read once from each engine's own allocation log,
not sampled across reps, so no spread, clock, or thermal effect applies.

**Correct claim:** *three nodes pool ~2.1× the KV cache of two.* Not 2.6×.

## 5. Why the TP=2 arm is missing

The orchestrator completed the TP=3 arm, stopped `dsv4.service` cleanly, rewrote both
2-node env files to the matched config, and passed the 2-node fabric gate 15/15. It then
died with a syntax error at line 260.

**Cause: operator error.** The script file was overwritten by `scp` *while bash was still
executing it*. Bash reads a script incrementally by byte offset, so the file shifting
underneath the interpreter corrupted the parse. Nothing was wrong with the cluster or the
data; the TP=3 bundle is complete and intact.

**Never overwrite a running script.** The resume
([`run_matched_2v3_tp2only.sh`](../scripts/run_matched_2v3_tp2only.sh)) is written to a
new path for exactly this reason, verifies every precondition rather than assuming it, and
restores `head.env`/`worker.env` from the `.prematched.*` backups before restarting the
3-node service.

## 6. What users should be told today

**On "should I run 3 nodes or 2?" — the honest answer is that this repository cannot yet
say, and its published numbers overstate the case for three.**

1. **Four of the five published decode deltas are not statistically significant.** They
   should not be quoted as wins. The `+16.7 %` headline in particular (p = 0.097).
2. **The one significant row (8K) is confounded** by three engine settings, so it is not a
   node-count result either.
3. **What is solid:** TP=3 works, is correct (14/14 acceptance, quality suite passing), is
   reproducible within ~±8 % across days, and serves 2K–262K without OOM. The KV pool is
   ~2.6× larger, though it has never been the binding constraint.
4. **What is unresolved:** whether three nodes decode faster than two at matched
   configuration. One arm remains.

**Recommended public position** until the TP=2 arm lands: *"Three-node TP=3 works, is
numerically correct, and is the shape we run. We are not currently able to claim a decode
speedup over two nodes — our own published deltas do not survive a significance test at
n=7, and the arms were not configuration-identical. A matched comparison is in progress."*

That is a weaker claim than the README makes today, and it is the one the data supports.
