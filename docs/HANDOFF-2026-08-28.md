## Key Takeaways for Future Operators

1. **Do not raise MTP to 5**: $K=5$ incurs a verification tax under batch load. $K=2$ ties single-stream decode within the noise floor (median stream 53.37 vs 54.32 tok/s, inside the 6.6–11.7% spread from #31) and wins aggregate throughput at concurrency.
2. **Do not raise `MAX_NUM_BATCHED_TOKENS` to 16384**: 8192 is the optimal physical sweet spot for DGX Spark LPDDR5X unified memory and point-to-point RoCE.
3. **Do not expect bit-for-bit parity at `<1e-6`**: Blackwell B12X MoE kernels are non-deterministic by design (~7–12% per-passage noise floor); use `--tolerance 12.0%`.
4. **Use Automatic Prefix Caching**: The warm path reduces TTFT from 78 seconds to **0.73 seconds** for multi-turn sessions.
